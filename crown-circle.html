Leadership giving

The Crown Circle

Some people are in a position to do more, and they are looking for the right place to put it. The Crown Circle is that place - a small, close community of leadership donors who help us sponsor a child's year of therapy, equipment, and dignity. Not a logo wall. A promise kept to real families.

Why a gift at this level matters here

Most of what a special-needs family needs falls into a gap. Insurance covers some therapy, but not enough of it. A state waiver might fund a piece of equipment, but the waitlist is measured in years. A grant covers one thing, once. In between all of that is a parent doing math at the kitchen table, deciding which of their child's needs can wait.

Teagan's Crown exists to stand in that gap. A leadership gift does not just add to a pile - it changes the size of what we can promise. It is the difference between helping a family this month and telling a child, over and over, to wait. When you give at this level, you are underwriting the part of a child's life that no program will name and no waitlist will reach in time.

Every child wears a crown. The Crown Circle is the community that makes sure the ones who need us most never have to wear it alone.

This page lays out how leadership giving works with us - the named giving levels, the idea of sponsoring a child's year, monthly leadership giving, and an honest account of the ways to give that are live today versus the ones we are still building. We would rather tell you exactly where we are than dress it up.

You can give a child a year without a waitlist.

Sponsor a child's year

One family. One year. Fully carried.

Leadership gifts let us commit to a child for a whole year instead of a single moment. Costs are real and they vary from family to family, so we will always be straight with you about where your gift goes. Here is the kind of weight it can help carry.

A year of therapy

Physical, occupational, speech, or feeding therapy is where progress actually happens - and where families run out of covered visits fastest. A sponsorship helps keep a child in the sessions that keep them moving forward, not on a pause that costs them ground.

Equipment that fits

Gait trainers, adaptive seating, communication devices, and safe sleep and mobility gear are what let a child join their own life. They are expensive, they get outgrown, and waivers move slowly. Leadership giving helps us close that gap before a child stalls waiting for it.

Dignity and belonging

The things that do not show up on a medical bill - a summer program that welcomes their child, a break for an exhausted caregiver, a moment of feeling like a kid and not a diagnosis. A sponsored year makes room for the parts of childhood every crown deserves.

Monthly leadership giving: the Standing Circle

Many of our strongest supporters give the same way the need arrives - steadily, month after month. Monthly leadership giving is the most useful gift you can make, because it lets us promise a family that help will still be there next month, not just this one.

A leadership-level monthly commitment - the kind that adds up to a Crown Bearer or Guardian gift across the year - turns your generosity into something a child can count on. It is easier on your own planning, and it is the closest thing we have to telling a parent: you are not going to run out of us.

Set it up your way

Prefer to give monthly? You can start a recurring gift on our giving page, or reach out and we will help you set up a leadership-level monthly commitment and point it toward the kind of need that matters most to you.

More ways to give

Bigger gifts, told to you straight.

There are powerful ways to give beyond a check or a card - through a donor-advised fund, through your estate, or matched by your employer. Some of these depend on our tax status, and we are early. Here is exactly what is live today and what is still in setup. No spin.

Donor-advised funds (DAF)

A donor-advised fund lets you recommend grants to charities from a fund you have already set aside for giving. It is one of the most tax-smart ways for leadership donors to give.

Where we are: DAF grants are typically made to organizations with confirmed 501(c)(3) recognition. Because our 501(c)(3) status is in progress, DAF giving to Teagan's Crown will open once that status - or a qualified fiscal sponsor - is in place. If you would like to give through a DAF, reach out and we will tell you honestly the moment it is available and how to route it.

In setup

Planned and legacy giving

Some supporters want their care for these children to outlast them - through a gift in a will, a beneficiary designation, or another planned vehicle. It is one of the most meaningful ways to be part of the Crown Circle.

Where we are: Certain planned-giving vehicles depend on confirmed 501(c)(3) recognition, so those will be available once our status or a fiscal sponsor is in place. We would be honored to talk with you and your advisor now about your intentions, and we will never ask you to finalize anything before the right structure exists. Please consult your own attorney or financial advisor on what fits your situation.

In setup

Corporate matching

Many employers will match their people's charitable gifts, sometimes doubling what you give. It is one of the simplest ways to increase your impact at no extra cost to you.

Where we are: Most matching programs require the receiving organization to have confirmed 501(c)(3) status, so employer matches to Teagan's Crown will generally become available once our status or a fiscal sponsor is in place. If your company offers matching, ask us - we will help you check your program and be ready to complete a match the moment we can.

In setup

Where we truly stand

We will always tell you the honest state of things.

Teagan's Crown is young, and we are building it the right way. Our 501(c)(3) status is in progress - we are not yet a registered or approved 501(c)(3). Because of that, we cannot tell you that a gift is tax-deductible, and we will not pretend otherwise. When our status - or a qualified fiscal sponsor - is in place, we will say so plainly and let every donor know.

We are sharing this on the same page where we ask you to give at a higher level on purpose. Trust is the whole thing. If you are considering a leadership gift, please talk with your own tax or financial advisor about your situation, and know that we will keep you current as our status changes. What we can promise today is simple: your gift goes to real children and real families, and we will show you where.
