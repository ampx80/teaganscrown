Family Assistance

Ask us for help. You are not alone.

If you're raising a child with special needs and you've hit a wall, this is a place to tell us what's happening. We're a small, new nonprofit, and we read every request personally.

Who this is for & what we can help with

  • Adaptive and mobility equipment — wheelchairs, walkers, standers, adaptive seating
  • Respiratory and medical devices, supplies, and therapy equipment
  • Vehicle and home accessibility — ramps, lifts, repairs, modifications
  • Medical bills, therapy costs, and prescriptions insurance won't cover
  • Family relief — the small, urgent things that help you keep going

We're a young organization with limited funds, so we can't say yes to everything, and we can't promise a grant. What we can promise is that a real person will read what you send with care — and if we can't help directly, we'll try to point you somewhere that can.
